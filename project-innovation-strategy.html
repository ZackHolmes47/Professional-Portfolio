Overview

Turning broad ideas into a real direction.

This project focused on addressing food insecurity in West Virginia nonprofits, where limited funding, small staff, and resource constraints make it difficult to meet demand.

The goal was to understand the problem from both a data and operations perspective and ifentify a realistic way to improve support for food pantries.

Approach

How the recommendation was built.

  • Defined the target areas, problems, and clarified who would be impacted.
  • Compared options based on feasibility, impact, and execution effort.
  • Utilized Tableau to create rich and descriptive data visualizations.
  • Presented the final direction in a concise, decision-ready format.
